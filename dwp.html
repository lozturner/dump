2026

The one number that matters

They're checking capital, not spending habits. Stay under £6k and there's nothing to explain. £6k–£16k reduces your award. Over £16k and means-tested benefits stop — PIP is not affected.

Capital limits

Under £6,000No impact — carry on.
£6k – £16kUC reduced: £4.67/month per £250 over.
Over £16,000Means-tested benefits stop.
PIPNot means-tested — unaffected by savings.

Red flags they look for

Regular depositsLooks like undeclared income.
Savings creeping upMay suggest hidden income source.
Large one-offs£2k+ drops — even legit ones get queried.
Account transfersCurrent ↔ savings ↔ PayPal looks like multiple incomes.
Online sellingEtsy/eBay/cash-in-hand treated as income if consistent.

What they are NOT checking

ShoppingIceland vs Aldi is not their business.
SubscriptionsNetflix, phone bill — not flagged.
TakeawaysYour pizza choices are legally off-limits.
LifestyleThey're checking eligibility, not behaviour.

Your rights

Ask why"Why do you need this? What are you looking for?"
Ask scope"Is this request necessary? Am I under suspicion?"
Data protectionThey must have a reason and follow GDPR.
ComplaintIf it feels wrong, raise it with DWP directly.

If they ask for statements

FormatOfficial PDF from bank — full name + account visible.
ScreenshotsRisky — avoid if possible.
RedactingPersonal info OK. Blocking amounts = suspicion.
If unsureAsk them exactly what they need before sending.
Keep copiesEverything you send, keep a copy.
